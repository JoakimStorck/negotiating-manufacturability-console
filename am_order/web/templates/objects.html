The product's objects

The product's features as the owner sees them. A place is a box (box x0 y0 z0 x1 y1 z1) or a cylinder (cylinder px py pz dx dy dz radius length, the point the centre of its base) in the part's coordinates, mm, one line per instance. The tool draws nothing: a place shows as its numbers and its checks.

Each object is fixed, an interface the design keeps, or free, the design may change around it (the part free: its shape). The design level derives from the binding which of a design's parameters that frees.
